Swarm Orbit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Swarm Orbit is a browser game. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no login. We do not ask you for your name, your e-mail address, or any other detail that identifies you, and we have no way of learning them from the game.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress — levels cleared, upgrades bought, settings, language — is saved in your browser (local storage, or the save system of the portal you are playing on). It stays on your device, is not readable by us, and clearing your browser data deletes it. That save also holds a random identifier (a number generated on your device the first time you play) and the date you first played, so the game can tell a returning player from a new one. The identifier is random: it is not derived from anything about you or your device, and it cannot be traced back to a person.

What the game sends to us

While you play, the game sends us anonymous gameplay and performance events so we can see where the game is confusing, too hard, or too slow. These events are written to our own server (sx.gamestao.com, run by us on Cloudflare) and, on some portals, also to the portal's own analytics. One event is a line like "wave 6 cleared at 03:12" or "frame rate 24".

CategoryWhat is in it
GameplayWhich level and wave you are on, what you bought or picked, waves cleared, runs started and ended, whether a run was won or lost, when you stopped playing, how long the session lasted.
PerformanceFrame rate, loading times, graphics quality tier, errors thrown by the game.
DeviceBrowser user-agent string, operating system, screen and window size, device pixel ratio, touch support, number of CPU cores, approximate memory, graphics card name reported by the browser, connection type, time-zone offset, browser languages, and the origin (domain only) of the page you came from.
SessionThe random identifier described above, a per-session identifier, the game version, and timestamps, so the events of one play session can be read in order.

Your IP address reaches our server, as it does with any web request. It is used only to work out the country your session came from and to limit abusive traffic; it is never written to storage. The stored row keeps the two-letter country code, nothing more.

The game sends no free-text fields, so nothing you type or that your machine names can end up in our data. We do not use this data for advertising, we do not build profiles, and we do not track you across other websites.

Why we are allowed to do this

We process this data on our legitimate interest in understanding how the game performs and in keeping it working on a wide range of devices (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The data is anonymous: we cannot identify you from it, and we make no attempt to.

How long we keep it

Raw event data is kept for at most 12 months and then deleted. Aggregated figures, such as "how many players reached level 5 in version 0.0.42", may be kept longer; they contain no identifiers.

Who else is involved

  • The portal you are playing on (for example CrazyGames or Playgama) hosts the page, serves the advertisements, and has its own privacy policy and cookie settings, which govern everything the portal itself does — including advertising. We have no access to the data a portal collects about you, and it has none to ours.
  • Cloudflare hosts the game files and our collection endpoint and processes data on our behalf.

Advertisements shown around or inside the game come from the portal, not from us. We do not place advertising cookies and we receive no advertising identifiers.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects nothing that identifies anybody, children included.

Your rights

You can clear everything the game stored on your device by clearing your browser data for the page. Because the data we receive is anonymous and holds no account, we cannot look up, export or delete "your" rows on request — there is nothing in them that ties a row to you. If you have a question about this policy, you can reach us through the support channel of the portal you are playing on, which will forward it to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
