Art style: premium stylized 3D mobile game art, like Clash Royale and Brawl Stars but more menacing: chunky readable shapes, hand-painted colors, soft top-left light, crisp soft shadows. Sci-fi alien insects in the spirit of Starship Troopers and StarCraft Zerg, aggressive and dangerous, never cute, no big cartoon eyes, no gore.
Enemy concept sheet seen from HIGH ABOVE, camera looking almost straight down at about 60 degrees, the way the player sees them in a top-down tower defense game. Plain flat icy slate-grey background. Seven creatures in two rows, each with its name in small text below. They share one family look: glossy dark purple-black segmented chitin, sharp edges, and glowing hot orange light that shows only through cracks and seams between armour plates and along stripes on the back (no round glowing circle on the back, no eyes on top). Every body is elongated head-thorax-abdomen, pointing up the page, with mandibles at the front so the direction of travel is obvious, and legs splayed wide outwards so they are visible from above. Each must be recognizable from its top-down silhouette alone at 35 pixels:
1. Swarmer: small, thin and long like an ant, six spindly splayed legs, two short mandibles.
2. Carapace: wide flat armoured beetle, a broad shield-shaped back plate wider than long, thick stubby legs poking out at the sides, big crushing pincers.
3. Flyer: slim dragonfly-like body with four long translucent violet wings spread flat in an X, legs tucked.
4. Sieger: slow heavy crawler carrying a long ridged spiral horn-shell on its back, pointing backwards, glowing seams along the spiral.
5. Acid blob: a lumpy translucent violet slime sac with dark nuclei inside and a few short tendrils, glowing from within; a smaller one beside it.
6. Leaper: narrow body with two huge bent hind legs folded high at its sides like a mantis-grasshopper, scythe forelegs, folded pointed wings.
7. Brood queen boss: three times bigger than the others, long segmented body, crown of horns, huge scythe forelegs, swollen glowing egg-sac abdomen trailing behind.
