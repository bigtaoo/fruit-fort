# The boss wave is the dead wave (2026-11-17)

Simulated, not telemetry. Tool: `scripts/wavetime.ts` with `TRAIN=1` (the training a player would
actually arrive with, `scripts/arriving.ts`, now shared with `scripts/ladder.ts`), hand policy,
8 seeds, wagons on. Follows up on `2026-11-12-level1-pacing.md`, whose live quit signal — median
quitter at 3.4 min, wave 6-7, fence 89-94%, `quiet` p50 5-6 s, i.e. still tapping — was read again
after 1-1 was rebuilt (11-12) and chapters 1-2 rescaled (11-14/15).

## What was measured

Every level ends on a boss wave, and it was the one wave that stood still:

| level | normal wave | boss wave | of it with <=2 bugs on the field |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | 18-25 s | 64.0 s | 4% (2.4 s) |
| 1-3 | 17-23 s | 66.9 s | 6% (4.0 s) |
| 1-5 | 12-18 s | 58.0 s | 39% (22.8 s) |
| 1-7 | 13-21 s | 57.1 s | 35% (20.0 s) |
| 1-10 | 13-21 s | 42.4 s | 39% (16.5 s) |
| 2-1 | 17-22 s | 47.7 s | 45% (21.4 s) |
| 2-10 | 18-24 s | 54.6 s | 46% (25.2 s) |
| 3-10 | 18-25 s | 53.7 s | 49% (26.6 s) |

Two shapes, one wave. From 1-5 on, nearly half of it is a dead board. On 1-2 and 1-3 the board is
full the whole time and the wave is simply long - a trained player's dps against a boss at `hp` 0.9
is just slow - which is the same minute of the player's life.

On the seven-wave levels 1-2 and 1-3 the boss wave **is** wave 7 (71.1 s and 74.1 s untrained,
64-67 s trained), and the levels landed at 3.4-3.5 min on the player's clock — past the measured quit point. So "wave 6-7"
was not stale after the 11-12 fix: the drag moved from the back half of 1-1 to the last wave of
every level, where it sits right before the reward.

Three causes, all in code: `waves.ts` gives a boss wave `ceil(many / 2)` ordinary bugs (half), the
boss spawns at `t + 1` so every escort is dealt with before he walks, and `LOCUST = { every: 6 }`
meant the king's three locusts were shot the moment they landed with the next batch 6 s away.

## What was changed

- `LOCUST.every` 6 -> 3, globally. Dead time, with the boss's health still untouched: 1-5 21.6 s ->
  4.6 s, 3-10 26.6 -> 14.0. Peak bugs on
  the wave unchanged (they die as fast as they come), and endless is untouched: `endless-prof.ts`
  reaches wave 47 at 845 s against 853 s, same peaks, same step times.
- `LevelDef.boss` (new): the boss's health times `hp` and the wave curve. Chapter 1 gets 0.55;
  chapters 2 and 3 keep 1, so the ladders tuned on 11-14 and 11-17 do not have to be redone.

Rejected after measuring, because they are difficulty changes and not pacing ones: `every: 2`
(3-10 fence 28% -> 9%), `hpShare` 0.045/0.08, and halving the escort split / leading with the boss
(`WAVES.bossEscort`/`bossAt`, built and reverted — it moved the dead share by 4 points at best,
raised peak bugs 14 -> 21, which the soft quality tier cannot afford, and pushed 1-1 from 2.7/3.2 min
to 2.9/3.5 min because 1-1 already carries `swarm: 3`).
